About Us

A professional service company with an operations-first mindset

HBR Electro Mechanical Services, Cargo Handling Services and Trading is based in Mariveles, Bataan, Philippines and is positioned as a dependable partner for clients who need technical capability, practical support, and a professional standard across service delivery and supply.

Who we are

Established in 2021, HBR supports vessel, industrial, and cargo-related environments with electro-mechanical services, cargo handling support, manpower deployment, and access to critical supplies that keep work moving.

Mission and vision

HBR’s mission is to be a leading electrical-mechanical and cargo handling services provider across industries through technical expertise, practical solutions, and sustainable opportunities. Its vision centers on exceptional workmanship, innovative solutions, and minimal disruption for clients.

What sets us apart

A growing 250+ insured workforce, a workshop-based operating setup, and a culture built on professionalism, teamwork, concern for people, and reliable execution in the field.
