Services

Technical support designed for operational continuity

HBR helps clients keep systems moving with practical support across electro-mechanical work, cargo handling operations, equipment upkeep, manpower supply, and technical material requirements.

Electro-Mechanical Services

General engine reconditioning, overhauling and repair, AC/DC motor reconditioning and rewinding, gear pump overhauling, diesel generator troubleshooting, and replacement of defective parts.

02

Cargo Handling and Manpower Support

Loading and discharging of all types of cargo, supply of manpower, stevedoring and arrester services, and bagging of products such as soya, wheat, rice, and corn.

03

Instrumentation, Piping, and Hot Works

Instrumentation support, troubleshooting and calibration, shaft RPM monitoring support, steering gear and servo system troubleshooting, piping, valve overhauling, reflating, and fabrication.

Supplies and Responsive Project Support

Flexible support for one-time work, recurring maintenance, urgent technical needs, and supply of technical materials and components required in the field or for workshop execution.
